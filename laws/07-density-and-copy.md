# 07 · Density and copy

## No cricket text

Nothing under 12.5px. No gray text on gray surfaces. Secondary text at 3:1 or better.

## No redundancy

Say a thing once on a screen. If the section is titled "Colors", the swatch labels are "Main", "Secondary", "Background", not "Main color". If a pill says "In every plan", the helper text does not repeat it.

## Chunk it

One idea per section. One row of numbers at most. One call to action at most. Numbered chapters with thin-rule headers let a reader scan a long page the way a table of contents reads.

## Fifth-grade how-tos

Instructions, onboarding copy and help text read at a fifth-grade level. Short words, short sentences, no jargon. The engineering room can keep its jargon; readers get plain words.

## Filter outbound copy

Before any copy reaches a human outside the team, run it through a slop filter: no filler openers, no hedging, no stacked adjectives, no "delve" or "leverage", no sentence that only comments on the previous sentence.

## Confirm tags

When a number or claim needs sign-off before it is public, tag it in place with a small amber "confirm" pill. The tag is the receipt that the decision belongs to someone else.
